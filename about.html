THE ENDURING PLATFORM

About ICERIA

ICERIA is the International Conference and Expo on Research and Innovations in Agriculture — a continuing platform for connecting research, innovation, partnerships, enterprise, investment and impact.

WHO WE ARE

A platform designed for continuity

ICERIA brings together researchers, innovators, policy leaders, development organisations, agribusinesses, investors, media and knowledge partners around agricultural research and innovation.

The platform is enduring. Each edition has its own programme, theme and event experience while contributing to the larger ICERIA institutional record.

OUR PURPOSE

From evidence to practical impact

ICERIA creates spaces where research outcomes can be understood, demonstrated, connected to enterprise and discussed in relation to commercialisation and adoption.

Research

Evidence & discovery

Highlighting agricultural research outcomes and the people behind them.

Innovation

Ideas into application

Showcasing technologies, approaches and innovations with practical potential.

Partnerships

Collaboration for scale

Connecting institutions, development partners, enterprise and knowledge networks.

Impact

Food systems & growth

Keeping the conversation focused on adoption, food sufficiency and economic growth.
