About ICERIA

The International Conference and Expo on Research and Innovations in Agriculture.

THE PLATFORM

The enduring ICERIA platform

ICERIA is a conference and expo platform connecting agricultural research, innovation, policy, enterprise, investment, knowledge and practical application.

Its purpose is to create a continuing space where research outcomes and innovations can be discussed, communicated, connected to enterprise and considered for practical application.
