RESEARCH MEETS ENTERPRISE

ICERIA Expo

The Expo is designed to make research and innovation visible, understandable and easier to connect with enterprise, investment, policy and adoption pathways. The categories below describe the Expo ecosystem and participation opportunities; they are not a list of confirmed 2027 exhibitors.

  • Research institutions and universities
  • AgriTech and digital agriculture innovators
  • Seed, input and production technologies
  • Food systems and agribusiness enterprises
  • Finance, insurance and investment organisations
  • Development programmes and knowledge partners
