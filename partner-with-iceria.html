COLLABORATION OPPORTUNITIES

What partnership can support

Participation can help strengthen the movement of evidence and innovation from research toward practical impact.

  • Research dissemination and knowledge exchange
  • Innovation visibility and technical dialogue
  • Commercialisation conversations and enterprise connections
  • Adoption and practical food-systems dialogue
  • Institutional networking and strategic collaboration
  • Expo participation and innovation showcase
  • Media engagement, promotion and partner visibility
  • Partnerships supporting ICERIA 2027 and its wider platform
