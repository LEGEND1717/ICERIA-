THE JOURNEY

Building on an established platform

ICERIA's archive brings together event materials, photographs and documented participation from previous editions. It is designed as a growing historical record rather than a simple photo gallery.

2019

ICERIA 2019 — Maiden Edition

24 October 2019
Nigerian Institute of International Affairs, Victoria Island, Lagos.

Theme: Harnessing Sustainable Agricultural Innovations for Economic Development.

The inaugural edition connected agricultural research, innovation, policy, enterprise, investment, communication and practical application, with an exhibition component.

View 2019 photographs →
2020

ICERIA 2020 — Second Edition

3 September 2020

Theme: Aggregating Sustainable Agricultural Innovations for Food Security.

The second edition was presented as a digital/virtual conference, bringing together speakers and participants around agricultural innovation and food security.
