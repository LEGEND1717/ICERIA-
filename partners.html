PEOPLE & PARTICIPATION

Selected historical representatives

Netherlands representation

Mr. Bryan Udoh — Agriculture Policy Officer, Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands — participated at ICERIA 2019 as presenter and panelist. The ICERIA 2020 visual archive also records Bram Wits, Agricultural Counselor for West Africa.

IITA representation

ICERIA 2019 documentation records IITA participants including Katherine Lopez, Yinka Fasogbon, Osunde Timilehin, Victor Saleh, Oyeyemi Azeez, Paul Emmanuel, Oiwoja Odihi and Tunde Sulaiman. Victor Saleh presented on breakthroughs and innovations in agricultural research and participated in the commercialisation panel.

Historical participation is part of the ICERIA institutional record. ICERIA 2027 partner status will be published when formally confirmed.
