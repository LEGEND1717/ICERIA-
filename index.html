PARTNERSHIPS & INSTITUTIONAL MEMORY

People and institutions that have been part of the journey

Historical participation is distinguished from confirmed 2027 partnership status.

Netherlands representation

Mr. Bryan Udoh, Agriculture Policy Officer, participated at ICERIA 2019; the 2020 visual archive also records Bram Wits, Agricultural Counselor for West Africa.

IITA participation

ICERIA 2019 included Victor Saleh of IITA-BIP, who presented and participated in the commercialisation panel. IITA's documented delegation also included Katherine Lopez, Yinka Fasogbon, Osunde Timilehin, Oyeyemi Azeez, Paul Emmanuel, Oiwoja Odihi and Tunde Sulaiman.

Historical relationships and participation are presented by edition. ICERIA 2027 partner status is published only when formally confirmed.
