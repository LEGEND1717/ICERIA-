THE ICERIA JOURNEY

2019 → 2020 → 2027: An enduring platform

ICERIA began as a physical conference and expo focused on agricultural research, innovation and the need for research outcomes to reach practical users. The 2020 edition extended that conversation into a digital format. ICERIA 2027 carries the platform into a new chapter with a sharper emphasis on commercialisation, adoption and impact.

The journey is therefore not simply a sequence of editions. It is a continuing effort to connect research, innovation, institutions, enterprise, investment and the people who ultimately use agricultural solutions.

RESEARCH TO IMPACT

What happens when research does not reach the user?

A research outcome can be scientifically valuable without yet being accessible, affordable, validated or adopted. Moving from knowledge to practical value requires connections between researchers, innovators, users, enterprises, financiers, policymakers and institutions.

ICERIA 2027 therefore frames the conversation as a pathway: Research → Innovation → Validation → Commercialisation → Adoption → Impact. The purpose is not to reduce research to commercial value, but to examine the systems and partnerships that can help useful outcomes travel further.

A GLOBAL PLATFORM

Connecting research, innovation, enterprise and investment

Agricultural transformation involves more than research institutions alone. It depends on relationships across farmers, agribusinesses, universities, technology providers, financial institutions, investors, government, development organisations and knowledge communities.

ICERIA provides a meeting point for these perspectives through conference dialogue, research and innovation presentations, exhibition, networking and partnership opportunities.

WHY PARTICIPATE

Why be part of ICERIA 2027?

Participation creates opportunities to exchange knowledge, showcase research and innovations, meet potential collaborators, explore enterprise and investment conversations, and understand the practical conditions for adoption.

Researchers can present evidence. Innovators can demonstrate solutions. Farmers and businesses can engage with technologies. Institutions can identify areas for collaboration. Investors and financial organisations can explore opportunities. Government and development stakeholders can connect policy and implementation perspectives.

THE NEXT CHAPTER

ICERIA 2027: From research and innovation to commercialisation, adoption and impact

ICERIA 2027 is built around a practical question: what can help valuable agricultural research outcomes and innovations move further toward use?

The 2027 theme — “CATALYSING COMMERCIALIZATION AND ADOPTION OF RESEARCH OUTCOMES AND INNOVATIONS FOR FOOD SUFFICIENCY AND ECONOMIC GROWTH OF AFRICA” — places commercialisation and adoption at the centre of the discussion while retaining research, innovation and food systems as the wider context.

WHAT THE JOURNEY HAS TAUGHT US

From conversations to pathways

The ICERIA record repeatedly returns to the distance between knowledge and use. A conference cannot close that distance by itself, but it can bring the relevant actors together, surface evidence, document lessons and create opportunities for further collaboration.

The next chapter therefore focuses on pathways — validation, enterprise, investment, commercialisation, adoption and measurable impact — alongside the research and innovation that generate new possibilities.
